How to play

  1. Tap a worm on the board.
  2. It moves in the direction its eyes are facing.
  3. If another worm blocks its way, answer an English question.
  4. A correct answer earns a star. A wrong answer loses one life.
  5. Help all the worms escape to finish the level and earn a garden medal!
