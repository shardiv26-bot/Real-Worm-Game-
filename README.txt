WORM GARDEN ADVENTURE - SJKT VIVEKANANDA

Chromebook instructions
1. Download and extract Worm_Garden_Adventure_Chromebook.zip using the Files app.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html to play in Chrome.
4. Keep index.html, styles.css and script.js together in the same folder.

GAME LEVELS
Beginner: 30 worms, 10 English questions, 7 lives (Flower Garden).
Intermediate: 45 worms, 10 English questions, 5 lives (Butterfly Garden).
Expert: 50 worms, 10 English questions, 3 lives (Mystery Forest).

FEATURES
- Animated student and teacher characters.
- Different colourful background for each level.
- Wiggly worms that move off the board when selected.
- 10 English questions in each level. Questions appear when a worm is blocked.
- Stars, confetti, and bronze/silver/gold garden champion badges.
- Hint, restart, home and change-level buttons.

The game works offline after the files are downloaded. The decorative Google Font may use a local fallback when offline.
